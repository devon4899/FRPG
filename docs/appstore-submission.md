## Description (draft)

> Your training is the game.
>
> RPGFit is a serious workout log wearing a fantasy quest's armor. Log real
> sets — warm-ups, RPE, supersets, per-set weights — and watch an honest
> character grow: XP comes only from training, never from purchases.
>
> THE LOG
> • Guided sessions with rest timer, Live Activity, and Save & Exit
> • Routines and repeat-last-workout
> • Per-exercise charts: est. 1RM, top set, volume, record timeline
> • Custom exercises, equipment-aware picker, plate calculator
> • Apple Health export (write-only — we never read your data)
>
> THE GAME
> • An optional Ledgerstone placement rite that calibrates your starting point and earns a training epithet
> • A campaign map of bosses your workouts damage
> • Companions that hatch, evolve, and train beside you
> • Gear earned from treasure chests — never sold
> • Daily and weekly quests that adapt to how you actually train
>
> THE PROMISE
> No ads. No account. No tracking. Your log never leaves your phone unless
> you back it up to your own private iCloud using CloudKit encrypted storage.

## Privacy questionnaire (App Store Connect → App Privacy)

- **Data collection: "No, we do not collect data from this app."** — true:
  no analytics, no accounts, no third-party SDKs. CloudKit private-database
  backup is user-initiated storage in the *user's own* iCloud and does not
  count as developer collection. HealthKit is write-only.
- Privacy manifest `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` already declares the required
  API reasons (UserDefaults CA92.1, file timestamps C617.1) and
  no-tracking. Keep the questionnaire consistent with it.

## Age rating

All questionnaire answers "None" → expected rating **4+**. (Fantasy combat
is abstract boss-bar damage; no realistic violence.)

## App Review notes (paste into Review Notes field)

> RPGFit needs no account or demo credentials. To reach the main experience,
> complete the seven-page onboarding: Get Started → Continue → choose exactly
> two goals → Reveal My Class → Continue → select a valid bodyweight → Approach
> the Ledgerstone. The four-question Weighing is optional; choose Pass the
> Stone Unread to skip it, wait for the Naming ceremony, then choose Start Your
> Journey. Training can be tested via Train → Begin Training → add "Push-Ups"
> → check a set → Finish Session. HealthKit is optional (Settings → Apple
> Health) and write-only. iCloud backup is optional and manual (Settings →
> iCloud Backup). The placement rite can be rerun from Settings → Return to the
> Stone.

Shot order for both device families:

1. Character sheet (identity plate, today, attributes)
2. Live session cockpit (sets checked, rest dock)
3. Campaign map / boss fight
4. Per-exercise charts
5. Satchel with waiting chests (loadout and set bonus live on Character)

Both sets were generated by `ScreenshotHarness` from freshly erased simulators
with parallel testing disabled. The harness fails rather than substituting a
fallback scene, proves the deterministic seed survives a terminate/relaunch,
and requires the real active-session controls and Bench Press chart before it
writes those files. Erase the target simulator (or freshly install the app)
before every future capture run.
